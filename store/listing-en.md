## Detailed description

```
Your AI conversations disappear into an endless sidebar you can't search. Vestige keeps them.

Every chat you open on Claude or ChatGPT is archived on your own computer, automatically. No button to press, no account to create, no server involved.

WHAT IT DOES

• Automatic archive — chats are saved as you read them. You don't have to remember to export anything.
• Import your whole history — bring in every past Claude conversation at once, without opening them one by one.
• Real full-text search — search across every conversation you've ever had, not just one. Filter by assistant, by date, by "contains code", or search only your own messages.
• Recover deleted replies — when you edit a message and regenerate, the old answer is gone forever. Vestige keeps it and shows it as an earlier version.
• "You asked this before" — start typing a question and, if you already asked something similar, a single quiet line appears above the composer with a link to that conversation. It works across assistants: it will remind you of a ChatGPT answer while you're typing to Claude.
• Folders and pinning — organise the conversations you keep coming back to.
• Markdown export — one conversation or your entire archive, as clean .md files.
• English and Turkish interface.

PRIVACY

Everything stays on your machine. Vestige has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. Your conversations are stored in your browser's local database and are never transmitted anywhere. The extension asks for exactly two site permissions — claude.ai and chatgpt.com — because those are the pages it reads your chats from. Nothing else.

WHY IT EXISTS

Most tools in this category are exporters: you open one conversation, download a file, and that's it. They don't remember anything. Vestige is the opposite — it's an archive that fills itself up and gets more useful the longer you use it.

Free. No premium tier, no upsell, no sign-up.
```
